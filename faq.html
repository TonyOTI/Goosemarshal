GooseMarshal.questions

What is Goosemarshal.com?

You don't Get to ask that question, you can't ask that!

Where is Goosemarshal.com?

Here, there, tomorrow, yesterday... he is where he needs to be, wherever a hOnkening needs happening.

Why is Goosemarshal.com?

You don't ask a goose why it honks or a UPS driver why they wear such short shorts! Nobody knows and we will never find Out.

How is Goosemarshal.com?

That's kind of you to ask. He's doing well. He finally caught up with his friend that he's been meaning to get in touch with. They would occasionally 'Like' each other's posts on social media and saw each other at a fantasy football draft where they said they were going to hangout more. Next thing you know it's been 8 years since that draft and life just "got in the way" as we tell ourselves. So much has changed. Goosemarshal has a child now and had to leave the fantasy league, he barely has friends at this point other than Goosemarsha.com but spouses don't count. All he really does is go to work, go home, or go to Goosemarshal.son's daycare and spends a few minutes each day making small talk with the staff. That's the only real social interaction outside of home that he gets these days. He used to have time to go to the bar with his pals from the war but things just aren't the same since the war ended. They grew apart. Flew apart. Goosemarshal.com. HONK!

Who is Goosemarshal.com?

Now you're honking up the right tree. Goosemarshal or Goosemarshal.com as he's formally known, is not like you or me, yet he is you *and* me. Legend tells it that he was first birthed from the cracking of the Liberty Bell. This is what the data tells us, at least, but it doesn't go unchallenged. There are microbiological traces of Goosemarshal dating back to the beginning of the Milky Way Galaxy. It's been thEorized by some of today's top scientists that the Liberty Bell Incident (or LBI) was ground zero of a worm hole, tearing reality and causality asunder, resulting in a time-defying being we can only refer to now as Mr. Goosemarshal.com. He arose as a warrior of life, liberty, and the pursuit of happiness.
