GooseMarshal.about

The Battle of The Bulge, Gettysburg, D-Day, Tet Offensive.
The names of famous battles go on, but that still doesn’t change the simple fact: Goose Marshal was there. He wasn’t there as a background actor, of course. He was there, time and time again, fighting on behalf of Americans, and most importantly, freedom. A timeless figure that while might be immortal, does have a beginning.

Flash back to 1840-something, Philadelphia. “Ring, ring,” the Liberty Bell sings as it had Many times before. Little did the Philly, and American, public know that this would be the last time this iconic symbol would make any sort of noise. “Ring, ring,” *CRACK*. Within seconds a deep crevasse tattooed itself along the copper side. It was the end of any sort of clamor out of the Liberty Bell, yet the birth of something else, something greater. Goose Marshal had taken his first flight.

With a triumphant honk, he flew off into the sunset. Merely seconds old and he knew exactly where he needed to go. When you are the embodiment of the freest nation to ever exist and time holds no bounds, you let the threat to liberty be your compass.

So began Goose Marshal’s eternal escapades. A time period here, a time period there. Always ending up where his services are required. Countless times the enemy had thought they won only for his mighty honk to strike fear into their hearts. His fearsome orange beak always snatching victory out of the jaws of defeat.

Fighting was his initial and main purpose. That doesn’t mean, though, that he didn’t find other purposes along the way. For instance, when he was in the Confederate South fighting on behalf of the Union, he found love.

Marching and honking through town, Goose Marshal was taken aback by a certain antebellum goose beauty. He shouldn’t have stopped, he couldn’t stop, but her gaze towards him made him grow another pair of wings. This time, around his heart.

He Approached her and asked what her name was. “Goose Marsha,” she replied. Ah, Goose Marsha. A beautiful name, the perfect name. She wasn’t happy. Her beliefs did not align with that of her family’s. She felt trapped to a life of falsehoods. In the past, and future, Goose Marshal felt the need to save a population from oppression. This time, he felt the need to save a person, nay, a goose. Goose Marsha desired him and he would soon find out, he desired her as well.

So began Goose Marshal’s life with his soulmate. Not long after, Goose Marsha gave birth to a healthy baby goose that they named Goose Marshal.son. Boy, did they ever love their son. Some say that Goose Marshal softened a bit when he became a husband and a father, but what they don’t realize is that he had to. He still worked and fought, but he also wanted to set a good example for his son who no doubt looked towards him as a role model. So he made time for them.

Since he could jump between time periods at will, Goose Marshal Recognized he could slow down. Take a vacation, have a quiet night in, cook a homemade meal with his family. He did all those things and the change of pace brought joy to his heart. Joy that previously he thought only life, liberty and the pursuit of happiness could bring.

When Goose Marshal.son was old enough, Goose Marshal decided it was time for them to further their bond by taking a father-son trip. It was going to be great, just a goose and his son flying through the skies together without a single care in the world. He didn’t realize it at the time, but this trip was about to become the biggest regret of his never-ending life.

It all began with a destination. When you can fly through time such as the Marshal family, you don’t simply vacation at different locations, but also different eras. An era Goose Marshal wanted to take his son to was one he rarely, if ever, frequented: the way, way future.

How exciting it all would be! The new technologies, take in all the quality of life improvements, just experience something new and unfamiliar with his boy.

Once arriving, though, his goose-gut told him Something was off. Flying through the air, Goose Marshal tasted almost a metallic quality... mechanical, even.

Everywhere he looked he kept on seeing the same billboard wherever he flew. To most unassuming eyes, the name on these billboards wouldn’t mean anything. To Goose Marshal, it sent shivers across his feathers. “QuackNet.” Everywhere, “QuackNet.” What was QuackNet?

Not important, didn’t matter. What did matter was the fact that He was on vacation with his son and they were going to enjoy it. So that’s what they continued to do until two mysterious machines met them in the sky.

They approached him and his son with an offer that Goose Marshal was sure they’d both refuse. “Let us enhance you with these breastal implants,” they bleep-blooped.

Goose Marshal insisted that they were fine and were just trying to enjoy a little vacation, but Goose Marshal.son interrupted.

“Actually dad, I think I want to give these implants a try.”

“Are you sure?”

“Yeah pops, I want to make you proud.”

“Son, I’m already proud of you, you don’t have to prove anything. Plus we don’t even know what these implants d—”

“Dad, just let me do this.”

“Ok son, I trust your judgement.”

In seconds, Almost invisible to the human, and goose, eye the machines inserted the implants into Goose Marshal.son’s chest, giving him the appearance of having boobs.

“Son,” Goose Marshal asked. “How do you feel?”

“I feel…” Goose Marshal.son began to say. “I feel… different.”

Before Goose Marshal could respond his son disappeared, vanished. “What’d you do with my son?!?!” Goose Marshal screamed. “Where is my son?!?!”

As he berated the mysterious machines, a blue-black blur scraped across the sky. Distracted, Goose Marshal’s attention shifted. What was that?

Before he could even process what he had just seen, a majestic black swan, with boobs, appeared in front of him.

“Finally,” the black swan, with boobs, said. “After billions of years our paths cross again.”

BilLions of years? Paths crossing again? What did this black swan, with boobs, mean?

“Who…who are you?” Goose Marshal demanded.

“They call me Señor Swan,” the swan, with boobs, said through a menace filled smirk. “But I’m surprised you don’t recognize me… father.”

“Fa—father?” Goose Marshal stammered. “What do you mean... father?”
